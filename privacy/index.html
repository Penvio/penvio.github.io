Penvio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Uraz Solutions Inc. ("we", "us", "our") handles personal information when you use the Penvio personal budget app for Android ("Penvio" or "the app") and the website https://penvio.github.io ("the website"). It is also our information notice under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Art. 13), Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK, Art. 10).

By using Penvio you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the app. Your use of the app is also governed by our Terms of Use.

Summary of key points

  • No account, no sign-up. Penvio never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your budget stays on your phone. Transactions, accounts, subscriptions, goals, debts, notes, categories and profiles are stored only on your device in an encrypted database. We cannot see them, and they are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No analytics. The app contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own.
  • Ads in the free version. Small Google AdMob ads appear at the bottom of the Reports and More screens. Google's ad software processes some device information (described below). Your financial records are never shared with the ad service.
  • "Remove ads" turns ads off completely. After the one-time purchase, no ads are shown and no ad requests are made.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You are in control. You can export, back up or delete all of your data from inside the app at any time.

2. What information we process

Information you enter in the app. Everything you record in Penvio (income, expenses, accounts and balances, subscriptions, installments, savings goals, debts and loans, spending limits, notes, tags, calendar events, categories and profile names) and your settings (theme, language, currency, notification preferences) are stored only on your device. This information never reaches us. We cannot access, view, change or recover it.

Information collected by the ad software (free version only). The Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app automatically collects the following and sends it to Google:

  • your Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system, language and app version);
  • ad and app interactions (such as ad impressions, taps and app launches);
  • diagnostic information (such as app launch time and hang rate).

Exchange-rate requests. When you tap "Fetch reference rate" while entering an amount in a foreign currency, the app sends only the two currency codes (for example "USD" and "CAD") to the Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.dev), which publishes European Central Bank reference rates. Like any internet request, this reveals your IP address to that service. The request does not reach us and we keep no record of it.

Purchase information. If you buy "Remove ads", the payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play gives us order information (such as order number, date, product, price and country). On launch, the app asks Google Play whether you have already bought "Remove ads" so it can restore the purchase.

Ratings. After you have used the app for a while, Google Play's in-app rating dialog may appear. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google Play under Google's terms; we only see what is published on Google Play.

Emails you send us. If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

Website. The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or ads. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may log technical information such as your IP address for security purposes under its own privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Information we do not collect. We do not collect your name, contacts, precise location, photos, bank login details or bank account data. Penvio does not connect to your bank. App lock uses Android's own fingerprint, face or screen-lock check; your biometric data is never passed to the app.

3. Why we process it and our legal bases

  • Showing ads in the free version, measuring them and preventing ad fraud. This lets us offer Penvio free of charge. See section 4 for the legal basis in each region.
  • Fetching the exchange rate you asked for. Legal basis: performing the service you requested (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Selling and restoring the "Remove ads" purchase, accounting and tax records. Legal basis: performing our contract with you and complying with our legal obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (ç)).
  • Answering your emails and support requests. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Complying with the law and protecting our rights, where required. Legal basis: legal obligation or legitimate interests.

In Canada, we rely on your consent as required by PIPEDA. Where the information is not sensitive and the purpose is clear (such as ads in a free app), that consent may be implied, and you can withdraw it as described in section 4.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Advertising and your choices

The free version of Penvio shows a small banner ad at the bottom of the Reports and More screens only. Ads are never selected based on your financial records, which never leave your device.

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: on first launch the app shows Google's consent form. Personalized ads, and storing or reading information on your device, rely on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). If you do not consent, only non-personalized or limited ads may be shown, and measuring them and preventing fraud rely on legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.
  • Türkiye: the app requests non-personalized ads only; ads are not selected based on your interests. Showing and measuring ads and preventing fraud rely on our legitimate interest in offering the app free of charge (KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Canada, the United States and other countries: Google may show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in Android settings and through Google's My Ad Center (https://myadcenter.google.com).

The simplest way to stop all ad-related processing is the one-time "Remove ads" purchase: after it, the app no longer requests ads.

Google processes ad data as described in its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we never share your budget records with anyone. The only third parties involved are:

  • Google (Google LLC, USA; Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland): ads (free version), in-app purchases, purchase restoration and ratings through Google Play.
  • Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev): receives only the currency codes of an exchange-rate request you start.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosts the website.
  • Authorities, courts or professional advisers, only if required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • A successor business, if we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. Any successor would remain bound by this policy for information it receives.

Note for California and other US states: under some US state privacy laws, allowing Google to show personalized ads may count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out as described in section 4, or by buying "Remove ads". We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

6. International transfers

We are based in Canada. Order information and emails that reach us are processed in Canada. Google, Frankfurter and GitHub may process information on servers in other countries, including the United States.

  • From the EEA, UK and Switzerland: the European Commission recognizes Canada as providing adequate protection for organizations subject to PIPEDA. Google relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions).
  • From Türkiye: transfers rely on the appropriate safeguards (standard contracts) provided for in KVKK Art. 9.

Your budget records are never transferred anywhere, except where you choose to save or share a backup or export file yourself.

7. How long we keep information

  • Records and settings on your device: until you delete them, use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Automatic backups on your device: only the 7 most recent are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Ad data: according to Google's retention periods (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  • Order information: as long as tax and commercial laws require (in Canada, generally six years).
  • Emails: deleted once your request is resolved, unless the law requires us to keep them.

8. How we keep information safe

  • The database on your device is encrypted with SQLCipher. Its key is kept in the Android Keystore.
  • Backup files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from a password you choose. Nobody, including us, knows that password, so a backup cannot be opened without it and we cannot recover a forgotten password.
  • If Android device backup to your Google account is turned on, the app's encrypted automatic backups and settings may be included in it. The backups remain unreadable without your password.
  • Ad, purchase and exchange-rate requests use encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Notifications (subscription renewals, pending payments, due dates, unusual spending) are created entirely on your device; no server is involved.

No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. Protecting your phone with a screen lock and turning on Penvio's app lock adds another layer of protection.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • have your information deleted;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

EEA, UK and Switzerland: you have the rights in GDPR Art. 15-21 listed above. You can complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work, or in the UK to the ICO (https://ico.org.uk).

Canada: under PIPEDA you can access your personal information, ask for it to be corrected and withdraw consent, subject to legal and contractual limits. You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (https://www.priv.gc.ca).

Türkiye: under KVKK Art. 11 you can learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred; ask for incomplete or inaccurate data to be corrected; ask for it to be deleted or destroyed under KVKK Art. 7 and for these actions to be notified to recipients; object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (https://www.kvkk.gov.tr).

United States: depending on your state, you may have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

How to exercise your rights:

  • Data on your device: it is already in your hands. Use Export (CSV or PDF) or Backup to get a copy, and Settings > Delete all data to erase all records, settings and automatic backups. Because we cannot access this data, we cannot show, change or delete it for you.
  • Ad data: use Settings > Ad privacy options (EEA, UK, Switzerland), reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or use https://myadcenter.google.com.
  • Everything else (order information, emails): write to info@zonepulse.ca. We respond free of charge within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity, for example by asking for your Google Play order number.
